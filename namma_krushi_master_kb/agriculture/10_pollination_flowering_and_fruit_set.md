# Pollination, flowering and fruit set

Tomato flowers are self-pollinating, but pollen still needs to be released effectively. In protected cultivation, low air movement, cloudy weather, very high humidity, heat stress or poor plant balance can reduce fruit set.

## Practical pollination support

Recent ICAR protected-tomato guidance notes that manual shaking, air movement or electric vibration can support pollination under cloudy or foggy conditions. The goal is gentle vibration of flower clusters, not physical damage.

## Why flowers drop

Blossom drop can result from excessive heat, temperature extremes, water stress, very high humidity, poor pollination, heavy vegetative growth, nutrient imbalance, root stress or pest/disease injury. It is therefore a symptom category rather than one diagnosis.

## Chatbot diagnostic sequence

Ask about greenhouse temperature during flowering, whether flowers are shedding before fruit formation, whether pollen appears powdery or sticky, whether plants are wilted, whether recent irrigation has been irregular, and whether the crop has excessive vegetative growth. Then retrieve the relevant environment, irrigation or nutrition article.

## Fruit set versus fruit enlargement

A flower can set fruit successfully and still abort later if the plant is under stress. Once fruit set occurs, steady irrigation and balanced nutrition become increasingly important as fruit load rises.
