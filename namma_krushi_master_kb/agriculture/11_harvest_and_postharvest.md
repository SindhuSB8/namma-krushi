# Harvest and postharvest

Harvest stage depends on market distance and intended use. Recent ICAR protected-tomato material distinguishes mature-green harvest for longer transport, pink-stage harvest for shorter movement, and fully red fruit for immediate consumption or processing. Local market preference should decide the practical stage.

## Harvest hygiene

Pick regularly, handle fruit gently, avoid dropping or compressing fruit, and use clean harvest containers. Remove damaged or diseased fruit from the production area rather than leaving it as a pest or pathogen source.

## Quality factors

Uniform colour, firmness, freedom from cracking, absence of blossom-end rot, pest injury and sunscald all affect market value. Many quality problems begin before harvest, so postharvest handling cannot correct poor irrigation or nutrition during fruit development.
