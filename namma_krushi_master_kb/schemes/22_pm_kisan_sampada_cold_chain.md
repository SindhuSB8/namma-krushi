# PM Kisan SAMPADA Yojana - Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure

This Ministry of Food Processing Industries scheme gives grant-in-aid for integrated cold chain and preservation infrastructure that connects the farm gate to the consumer, with components such as pre-cooling, collection centres, cold storage, refrigerated transport and primary processing. Assistance is roughly 35% of the eligible project cost in general areas and 50% in difficult and hilly areas, subject to a ceiling per project.

The scheme is driven by entrepreneurs, farmer producer organisations, cooperatives and companies. Stand-alone cold storages are not covered, because the project must be an integrated chain, and the applicant must bring the remaining investment through own funds and bank loans.

Applications are invited by the Ministry in rounds on its portal with a detailed project report, so check whether a call for proposals is currently open and read the latest guidelines before preparing a proposal.
