# Nursery and planting material support under MIDH

The Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture supports quality planting material through two kinds of nursery. A hi-tech nursery (2 to 4 hectares) has a cost norm of Rs 25 lakh per hectare, and a private-sector nursery gets a credit-linked back-ended subsidy of 40% of the cost, while public-sector nurseries receive full assistance. A small nursery (up to 1 hectare) has a cost norm of Rs 15 lakh per hectare, and private owners receive 50% as a credit-linked back-ended subsidy.

These nursery norms are written mainly for perennial fruit, spice and plantation planting material and expect a minimum annual output of certified plants. For a tomato seedling nursery the more usual routes are the assistance for raising vegetable seedlings in a polyhouse under the protected cultivation component, the National Horticulture Board's commercial horticulture project subsidy, and state programmes that supply tomato seedlings or protrays to farmers.

Apply through the State Horticulture Mission or the District Horticulture Office. Nurseries usually need accreditation, and the subsidy is paid after the bank loan is sanctioned and the nursery is built. Confirm which crops and sizes your state has approved in its current action plan.
