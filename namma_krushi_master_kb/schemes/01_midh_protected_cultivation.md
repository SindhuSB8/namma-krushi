# Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH)

MIDH is a centrally sponsored horticulture-development scheme covering fruits, vegetables and other horticultural crops. Protected cultivation is one of the areas covered through operational guidelines and component-based assistance.

The 2025 MIDH/NHB guidelines include protected-cultivation cost norms and assistance patterns, including components related to polyhouses, cultivation of high-value crops, mulching and certain add-on technologies. Exact admissible cost, beneficiary area, state implementation and assistance can change through notifications.

## Namma Krushi chatbot response pattern

When a farmer asks “Can I get subsidy for a polyhouse?”, answer that protected cultivation is supported under MIDH/NHB frameworks, then provide the official MIDH/NHB link and instruct the farmer to verify the current Karnataka implementation, eligible structure, area limit, category and application window with the Karnataka Horticulture Department.

Do not hard-code a single subsidy percentage as universally applicable. The applicable pattern depends on the component and current guidelines.
