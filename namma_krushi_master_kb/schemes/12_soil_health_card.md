# Soil Health Card Scheme

The Soil Health Card scheme gives farmers a free soil test and a card that reports the nutrient status of their soil with crop-wise fertilizer and nutrient recommendations. The aim is to use the right amount of fertilizer, cut input cost and keep the soil healthy. Cards are issued to farmers roughly once in every two years.

How to use it: give a soil sample from your field to the state agriculture department's soil testing laboratory, or contact the Raita Samparka Kendra or agriculture office of your taluk. The card lists nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium and other soil parameters and suggests how much fertilizer to apply for your crop.

For a greenhouse tomato crop grown directly in soil, the card helps plan basal and top-dress fertilizer. For soilless substrate (cocopeat) the soil card does not apply, and a substrate and irrigation-water test is the better guide.
