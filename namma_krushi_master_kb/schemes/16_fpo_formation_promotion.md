# Formation and Promotion of 10,000 Farmer Producer Organisations (FPO Scheme)

This central scheme helps farmers form and run Farmer Producer Organisations, which are farmer-owned companies or cooperatives that buy inputs, process and sell produce together. Acting as a group can lower input cost, give better bargaining power with buyers and make it easier to get credit and subsidies.

Support includes professional management support during the first years, an equity grant that matches the share capital contributed by member farmers, and a credit guarantee cover that helps the FPO get loans from banks without collateral. Implementing agencies such as SFAC, NABARD and NCDC help form and handhold the FPO.

Interested tomato growers should contact their district horticulture or agriculture office or an implementing agency to join an existing FPO or form a new one. A minimum number of member farmers is required. Check the latest operational guidelines for the equity grant and credit guarantee limits.
