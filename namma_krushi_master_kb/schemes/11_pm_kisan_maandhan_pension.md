# PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY) - pension for small and marginal farmers

PM-KMY is a voluntary, contributory pension scheme for small and marginal farmers. After the age of 60 the farmer receives a fixed pension of Rs 3,000 per month.

Eligibility: small and marginal farmers aged 18 to 40 years who own cultivable land up to 2 hectares. Farmers already covered by the National Pension System, ESIC or EPFO, and income-tax payers, are not eligible.

Contribution: the farmer pays a small monthly amount, roughly between Rs 55 and Rs 200 depending on the age of joining, and the central government contributes an equal amount to the pension fund. Contributions can be deducted automatically from the PM-KISAN instalment for farmers who are enrolled in PM-KISAN.

Enrol at a nearby Common Service Centre with your Aadhaar, land records and bank passbook, or through the official PM-KMY portal. Confirm the current contribution chart before joining.
