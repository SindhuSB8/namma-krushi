# PM Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PMFME)

PMFME supports individual micro food-processing enterprises, farmer producer organisations, self-help groups and cooperatives that want to set up or upgrade a processing unit. Tomato-based products such as puree, sauce, ketchup, paste and dried tomato are examples of processing a farmer or group can take up.

For an individual micro enterprise the scheme gives a credit-linked capital subsidy of 35% of the eligible project cost, up to a maximum of Rs 10 lakh per unit. The remaining cost comes from the applicant's own contribution and a bank loan. Training, branding and marketing support are also available to groups.

Applications are made through the official PMFME portal and the state nodal agency, and need a project proposal, identity and bank details, and for existing units the proof of operation. Check the current scheme period and guidelines before applying.
