# Operation Greens

Operation Greens is a central scheme of the Ministry of Food Processing Industries that was started in 2018-19 for the tomato, onion and potato value chain. Its short-term measure gives a 50% subsidy on the cost of transporting eligible crops from surplus production areas to markets or storage, and a 50% subsidy on hiring suitable storage, including cold storage, so that growers can avoid distress sales when prices crash. Its long-term measure supports integrated value-chain projects such as farm-gate infrastructure, processing and market links for clusters and producer organisations.

The scheme has been widened from tomato, onion and potato to a larger list of perishable fruits and vegetables. Payments are claimed with invoices and proof of payment for transport and storage, and are made to eligible applicants such as farmers, producer organisations, cooperatives and traders under the scheme guidelines.

The scheme has been reported to be under review in 2026, so check with the Ministry of Food Processing Industries or NAFED whether subsidy claims are open before relying on it.
