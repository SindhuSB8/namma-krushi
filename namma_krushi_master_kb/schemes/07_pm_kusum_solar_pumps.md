# PM-KUSUM (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan)

PM-KUSUM is a Government of India scheme run through the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE). It supports farmers to use solar energy for irrigation and farm income, and has three components: Component A for grid-connected solar power plants, Component B for stand-alone solar agriculture pumps, and Component C for solarising existing grid-connected agriculture pumps.

For an individual greenhouse or polyhouse tomato grower, Component B and Component C are the usual options. Component B helps replace diesel pumps or irrigation systems with stand-alone solar pumps of up to 7.5 HP where grid power is not available. Component C helps farmers who already have a grid-connected pump to solarise it.

Assistance is shared between the central and state governments and the farmer contributes the balance, but the exact subsidy percentage, benchmark cost, pump capacity limit, vendor list and application window depend on the state and on current MNRE notifications. The scheme period has been extended more than once, so confirm that applications are open before planning.

To check eligibility or apply, contact the Karnataka state nodal agency or the local electricity supply company office and use the official MNRE PM-KUSUM information. Do not assume eligibility from crop or sensor results alone.
