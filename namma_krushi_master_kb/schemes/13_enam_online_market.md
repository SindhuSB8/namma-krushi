# e-NAM - National Agriculture Market

e-NAM is a national online trading platform that links regulated agricultural markets (APMC mandis) so that farmers can see prices across markets and sell to a wider group of buyers. The produce is assessed, listed in the mandi and traded electronically, with payment going directly to the farmer's bank account.

For tomato growers, e-NAM can improve price discovery because bids can come from traders outside the local mandi. Prices of perishable produce such as tomato change quickly, so also compare the daily rate in your nearest market before deciding when to sell.

To use it, register as a farmer at an e-NAM linked mandi or on the e-NAM portal with your Aadhaar, mobile number, bank account details and land or identity proof. Not every mandi is integrated, so check whether your nearest market is an e-NAM mandi.
