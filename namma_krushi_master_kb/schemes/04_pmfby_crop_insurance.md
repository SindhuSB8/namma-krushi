# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

PMFBY is the national crop-insurance program administered by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. The official portal provides farmer enrollment, premium calculation, application status, policy-related tools, guidelines and loss/grievance support.

Whether tomato or another horticultural crop is insurable depends on notified crops, area and season. The farmer should check the current state/season notification rather than assuming every crop is automatically covered.

Important Karnataka note: the PMFBY portal currently states that direct online enrollment on the national portal is not available for Karnataka and Gujarat because those states enroll farmers through their own portals. The chatbot should therefore direct Karnataka farmers to the appropriate state route and official PMFBY information.
