# Telangana and Andhra Pradesh - polyhouse subsidies

Telangana and Andhra Pradesh have been reported as among the states giving the highest polyhouse support to farmers. Telangana's horticulture department was reported to give about 75% subsidy to general-category farmers and about 95% to SC and ST farmers for polyhouse cultivation of vegetables and flowers. Policies and budgets change, so these rates may differ now.

Applications are made through the State Horticulture Mission and the district horticulture officers, whose annual action plans list the protected cultivation units (greenhouses and shade nets), their cost norms and the targets for the year.

Contact your district horticulture office to confirm whether the component is open, the current subsidy rate for your category, and the documents required, such as land records, Aadhaar, bank details and a caste certificate where applicable.
