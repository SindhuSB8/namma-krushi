# Karnataka - polyhouse, shade net and horticulture subsidies

In Karnataka, polyhouse and shade-net subsidies are given through the Department of Horticulture, using the central MIDH and National Horticulture Mission norms. The base assistance is 50% of the admissible cost for up to 4,000 square metres per beneficiary. The department's published scheme list gives a higher 90% subsidy for Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe farmers, and 50% for small, very small and other farmers. Some reports state that state top-ups raise the support for general farmers further, so confirm the current rate at your District Horticulture Office.

Karnataka's Krishi Bhagya programme for rainfed districts has also been reported to support farm ponds and polyhouses, so ask about its current status.

Apply at the taluk or district horticulture office, usually with your FRUITS farmer registration ID, land records (RTC), Aadhaar, bank passbook, a vendor quotation, and a caste certificate if applicable. Construction should start only after the sanction order. Check which funds are open this year.
