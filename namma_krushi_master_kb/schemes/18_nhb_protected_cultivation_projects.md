# National Horticulture Board - Commercial Horticulture in protected cover

The National Horticulture Board (NHB) gives a credit-linked back-ended subsidy of 50% of the project cost, up to a maximum of Rs 56 lakh per project, for protected cultivation. Eligible items include greenhouses, shade net houses, plastic tunnels, anti-bird and anti-hail nets, plastic mulching and the cost of planting material for high-value vegetables such as tomato, all calculated on the admissible cost norms.

This route suits larger commercial projects than the per-farmer area limit of the state horticulture mission schemes. Individuals, groups of farmers, producer organisations, cooperatives and companies can apply. Rates and limits are higher in North-Eastern and hilly states.

Apply on the NHB portal with a detailed project report, land documents, quotations and a bank loan sanction. The subsidy is released after the project is completed and inspected, so do not start purchases before the application is accepted. Check the latest NHB guidelines because cost norms are revised from time to time.
