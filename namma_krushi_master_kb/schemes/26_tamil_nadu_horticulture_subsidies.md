# Tamil Nadu - horticulture subsidies

The Tamil Nadu Department of Horticulture and Plantation Crops runs the Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture. Official district pages describe 50% subsidy for building polyhouses at a rate of about Rs 467.50 per square metre, up to 4,000 square metres per beneficiary, with shade-net houses supported in the same way.

For hybrid vegetables including tomato, the same pages describe protray seedlings from state horticulture farms being supplied to farmers at no cost at a rate of Rs 20,000 per hectare. Micro-irrigation is promoted with 100% subsidy for small and marginal farmers and 75% for other farmers.

These details are taken from district pages published around 2020-21, so rates and the list of components may have changed. Apply through the Deputy Director of Horticulture in your district with land records, Aadhaar, bank details, and soil and water test reports where required.
