# Organic and natural farming support

Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY) promotes cluster-based organic farming. Groups of farmers form clusters, use organic inputs, and get certified through the Participatory Guarantee System (PGS-India), which helps them sell produce as organic. Financial help is given for inputs, training, certification and marketing.

The National Mission on Natural Farming, approved by the Union Cabinet in 2024, encourages chemical-free farming using locally prepared inputs and sets up bio-input resource centres and trained farmer volunteers.

For tomato growers who want to sell organic produce, these schemes help with certification and inputs, though they are better suited to open-field and group farming than to a single hydroponic or heavily fertigated greenhouse. Contact the district agriculture office to join a cluster and check the current per-hectare assistance and enrolment dates.
