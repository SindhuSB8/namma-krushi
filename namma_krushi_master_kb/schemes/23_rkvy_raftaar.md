# Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana - Remunerative Approaches for Agriculture and Allied sector Rejuvenation (RKVY-RAFTAAR)

RKVY-RAFTAAR is a central scheme that gives states flexible funds to strengthen agriculture, based on each state's own agriculture plan. Money is used for farm infrastructure, value-chain projects, and many state programmes that farmers know under other names, including help with greenhouses, shade nets, drip irrigation and post-harvest facilities. It also funds agri-startups and agri-business incubators that give grants and training to young agri-entrepreneurs.

A farmer does not apply to RKVY directly. The benefit reaches you as a component of the state agriculture or horticulture department programme, and agri-startups apply through the nodal incubators named by the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.

Ask your taluk agriculture or horticulture office which RKVY-funded components are open this year, and check the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare website for the startup and incubator calls.
