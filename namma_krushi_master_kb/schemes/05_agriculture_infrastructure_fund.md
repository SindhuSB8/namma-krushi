# Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)

AIF is a Central Sector financing facility intended to support viable post-harvest management infrastructure and community farming assets. Eligible entities include categories such as farmer groups, FPOs, cooperatives, agriculture entrepreneurs and other entities described in current guidelines.

The scheme is more relevant to infrastructure investment than to routine daily crop inputs. A tomato farmer or group may find it relevant for qualifying post-harvest or community assets, aggregation, storage or other eligible infrastructure projects.

The chatbot should not assume a greenhouse itself qualifies under AIF without checking current eligible-project definitions. For protected-cultivation subsidy questions, MIDH/NHB and the state horticulture department are usually the first sources to check.
