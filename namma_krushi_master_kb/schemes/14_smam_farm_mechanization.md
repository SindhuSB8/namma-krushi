# Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM) - farm machinery and tractor implement subsidy

SMAM helps farmers buy farm machinery and equipment at a subsidy, and helps groups set up custom hiring centres so small farmers can rent machines. Equipment covered includes tractor-drawn implements, power tillers, sprayers, planters and post-harvest machinery.

Assistance is usually a percentage of the machine cost. It is generally higher for small and marginal farmers, women, and SC/ST farmers than for other farmers, and is commonly in the range of about 40% to 50% of the cost, subject to a ceiling for each machine. The exact rate and approved machine list are decided by the state agriculture department.

In Karnataka, apply through the Department of Agriculture (Raita Samparka Kendra) or its mechanization portal. Normally you select an approved machine and dealer, get a quotation, and the subsidy is released after the purchase is verified. Check the current year's machine list and funds available.
