# Maharashtra - polyhouse subsidy and PoCRA

Maharashtra farmers apply for horticulture and polyhouse subsidies online through the MahaDBT portal under the Horticulture Department section. The base assistance under the National Horticulture Mission is 50% of the admissible cost, and some reports say the state adds a top-up that brings eligible categories to roughly 65%, with a ceiling per unit area, so confirm the current rate.

In drought-prone districts, the Nanaji Deshmukh Krishi Sanjivani Prakalp, known as PoCRA, supports climate-resilient practices and has components such as shade nets, polyhouses, drip irrigation and farm ponds for eligible villages.

Register on MahaDBT with Aadhaar, land records (7/12 extract) and bank details, select the scheme, and wait for pre-sanction before buying or building. Check whether your district and village are covered for PoCRA.
