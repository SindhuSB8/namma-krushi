# Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

KCC is designed to provide farmers timely credit for cultivation and related needs through a simplified credit framework. The myScheme page lists short-term crop requirements, post-harvest expenses, produce marketing, certain household consumption needs, maintenance of farm assets and investment credit for agriculture/allied activities within the scheme framework.

Eligibility includes owner-cultivators and can include tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers and eligible farmer groups subject to bank rules. The exact credit limit and documentation are determined by the lending institution and local scale of finance.

The chatbot should provide the official myScheme/bank route and avoid promising approval or a fixed loan amount.
