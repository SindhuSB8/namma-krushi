# National Horticulture Mission (NHM) under MIDH

The National Horticulture Mission is one of the sub-schemes that operate under the Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH). It supports area expansion, productivity improvement and post-harvest management for fruits, vegetables and other horticultural crops, and is implemented through state horticulture departments.

Component-based assistance is available for activities such as quality planting material, protected cultivation structures such as polyhouses and shade-net houses, drip irrigation and mulching, integrated pest management and post-harvest facilities. Assistance is normally a percentage of an admissible cost norm that varies by component, so the farmer's share depends on the structure and on the current guidelines.

In Karnataka, farmers apply through the Department of Horticulture (district and taluk horticulture offices). Typical requirements include land records, identity and bank details, and a proposal for the selected component. Beneficiary limits, categories and application periods change through notifications.

For a tomato polyhouse, first confirm that the component is open in your district, then check the approved cost norm and whether a technical approval or vendor registration is required before starting construction.
