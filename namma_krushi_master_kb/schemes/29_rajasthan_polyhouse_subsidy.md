# Rajasthan - polyhouse and shade-net subsidy

Rajasthan gives polyhouse and shade-net subsidies through the Horticulture Department using the Raj Kisan Saathi portal. Reports describe 50% subsidy for general farmers and higher support, up to about 75%, for SC/ST and small and marginal farmers, for up to 4,000 square metres per farmer.

Farmers log in with their Jan Aadhaar number, choose the Horticulture Department and apply for the green house or shade-net component, with land records and a quotation from an empanelled manufacturer. An important rule reported for Rajasthan is to wait for the Letter of Intent or administrative approval before starting construction, otherwise the project can be disqualified.

Confirm the present subsidy rates and empanelled manufacturers with the District Horticulture Officer before applying.
