# Gujarat - iKhedut portal for horticulture subsidies

Gujarat delivers most farm subsidies through the iKhedut portal of the state government, including horticulture schemes for protected cultivation, drip irrigation and farm machinery. Reported polyhouse support is 50% of the admissible cost, with state top-ups raising it for some categories and structures to as much as 65%, so confirm the current rate on the portal.

Applications are online. Farmers normally need an 8-A land record, an Aadhaar-linked bank account, a vendor quotation and, for larger structures, a detailed project report. Register on the portal during the period when the scheme is open and keep the acknowledgement, because approval order and inspection come before payment.

Check the portal for the list of components, the approved vendors and the dates on which applications open each year.
