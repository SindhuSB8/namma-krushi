# Haryana, Punjab and Himachal Pradesh - polyhouse support

In North India the central base subsidy for protected cultivation is 50% of the admissible cost under MIDH and NHB. Haryana is reported to add a state top-up of about 15%, giving up to roughly 65% in total, and applications are made through the Haryana Horticulture Department portal or the District Horticulture Officer.

Punjab is reported to give the 50% base subsidy with little additional state top-up through its Department of Horticulture. Himachal Pradesh is a hilly state where the central share is higher, and it has been reported to offer much higher state support for polyhouses and for replacing worn polyhouse sheets, but the rate depends on the category of the farmer and on the year.

Rates differ by year and by category, so treat these figures as a guide and confirm them with the state horticulture department before planning a project.
