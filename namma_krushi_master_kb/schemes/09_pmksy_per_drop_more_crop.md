# Per Drop More Crop (PDMC) - drip and sprinkler subsidy

Per Drop More Crop supports farmers to install micro-irrigation systems, namely drip and sprinkler irrigation, so that water reaches the root zone efficiently. It began under the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) and, from 2022-23, is implemented under the Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana.

Financial assistance is 55% of the eligible cost for small and marginal farmers and 45% for other farmers, limited to an overall ceiling of 5 hectares per beneficiary. Several states, including Karnataka, add their own top-up assistance, so the amount you finally receive can differ from these central percentages.

For a tomato greenhouse, drip irrigation with fertigation is the usual system covered. Apply through the Karnataka Department of Horticulture (for horticulture crops) or the Department of Agriculture at your taluk office. Typical requirements are land records, identity and bank details, a quotation from an empanelled micro-irrigation supplier, and installation by that supplier before the subsidy is released.

Check the current state guidelines for the approved unit cost, empanelled suppliers and the application window.
