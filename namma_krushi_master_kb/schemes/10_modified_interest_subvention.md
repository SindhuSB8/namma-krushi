# Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS)

MISS makes short-term farm loans cheaper for farmers who borrow through a Kisan Credit Card. Banks lend at 7% per year on short-term loans up to Rs 3 lakh, with the government paying banks a 1.5% interest subvention. Farmers who repay on time get a further prompt repayment incentive of 3%, which brings the effective interest rate down to about 4%.

The Union Budget for 2025-26 announced raising the loan limit from Rs 3 lakh to Rs 5 lakh. The scheme has been continued year by year, so confirm the limit and rates that apply to the current financial year with your bank branch.

The benefit is claimed through your lending bank or primary agricultural cooperative society. You need an active Kisan Credit Card account, land records and your bank details. Repaying within the due date is what earns the prompt repayment incentive.

For loans only for animal husbandry or fisheries, the interest benefit applies up to a lower limit of Rs 2 lakh.
