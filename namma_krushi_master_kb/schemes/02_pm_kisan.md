# PM-KISAN

PM-KISAN is a Central Sector scheme funded by the Government of India. The official portal states that eligible landholding farmer families receive income support of ₹6,000 per year in three equal instalments, subject to scheme eligibility and exclusion categories.

The state/UT identifies eligible farmer families and funds are transferred directly to beneficiary bank accounts. Exclusion categories apply to several higher-income or specified institutional/professional categories.

For eligibility checks, registration status or corrections, the chatbot should send the farmer to the official PM-KISAN portal because records and requirements are personal and time-sensitive.
