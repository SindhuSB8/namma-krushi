# Thrips

Thrips scrape plant tissue and can cause silvery streaking, bud damage and premature flower drop. They are also vectors of tomato spotted wilt virus.

## Monitoring

Inspect flowers and tender leaves, use appropriate sticky traps, and watch for silvery feeding scars. Because thrips can hide in flowers, leaf-only inspection may underestimate the population.

## Management

Keep weeds under control, use exclusion where practical, remove heavily infested material, conserve beneficial insects and use local IPM guidance for treatment decisions.
