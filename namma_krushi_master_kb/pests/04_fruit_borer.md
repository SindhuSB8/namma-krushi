# Tomato fruit borer

Fruit-borer caterpillars feed on foliage when young and later bore into developing fruit, creating holes and secondary rot. One larva can damage multiple fruits.

## Integrated management

Remove damaged fruit and visible larvae, monitor adult moths with pheromone traps, use trap-crop strategies such as marigold where locally recommended, support natural enemies and use need-based treatment rather than calendar spraying. ICAR-NRIIPM tomato IPM material emphasizes combining multiple tactics.
