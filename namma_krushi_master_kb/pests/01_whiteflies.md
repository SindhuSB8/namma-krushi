# Whiteflies

Whiteflies feed on leaf sap and can produce honeydew that supports sooty mould. More importantly in tomato, whiteflies transmit important leaf-curl viruses. Protected environments can allow populations to build quickly after entry.

## Monitoring and prevention

Use insect-proof nursery protection, inspect new seedlings, remove weed hosts, use yellow sticky traps for monitoring and keep greenhouse openings/exclusion systems in good condition. Inspect leaf undersides because immature stages are easy to miss.

## Management principle

Treat whitefly as both a direct pest and a virus vector. A low visible population can still matter when virus is present in the area. Use integrated management and current local recommendations rather than repeated unscheduled insecticide spraying.
