# Integrated pest management (IPM)

IPM combines prevention, monitoring and multiple compatible control methods. The objective is not “zero insects at all times”; it is to prevent economically damaging populations and disease-vector pressure while avoiding unnecessary pesticide use.

## Core components

1. Start with healthy seedlings and clean growing media.
2. Use insect-proof nursery protection and maintain greenhouse exclusion.
3. Keep weeds and crop residues under control.
4. Train plants so the canopy can be inspected.
5. Use sticky traps and pheromone traps for monitoring appropriate pests.
6. Use trap crops or barrier crops where locally validated.
7. Conserve or release beneficial organisms when part of the local program.
8. Remove severely infested or infected plant material.
9. Use pesticides only when justified and follow current registration, label, waiting period and resistance-management advice.
