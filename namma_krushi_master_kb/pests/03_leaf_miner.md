# Serpentine leaf miner

Leaf-miner larvae tunnel within leaves, producing winding or serpentine mines. Heavy injury reduces useful leaf area and can lead to drying and premature leaf drop.

## Management

Scout for fresh mines, remove heavily mined leaves when infestation is limited, avoid unnecessary broad-spectrum insecticides that disrupt parasitoids, and follow local IPM recommendations if populations increase.
