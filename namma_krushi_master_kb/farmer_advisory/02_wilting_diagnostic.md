# Wilting: diagnostic guide

First determine whether the plant recovers after the hottest part of the day. Temporary midday wilt with moist soil can be heat/transpiration stress. Persistent wilt needs deeper investigation.

## Check in this order

- Is the root zone dry, waterlogged or normal?
- Is the drip emitter working?
- Is only one irrigation line affected?
- Are roots brown or decayed?
- Does the cut stem show vascular browning or bacterial ooze?
- Are lower leaves yellowing first?
- Is greenhouse temperature extreme?

Persistent wilt despite adequate water can indicate Fusarium wilt, bacterial wilt, root damage or severe stem injury. Avoid automatically adding more water to already saturated soil.
