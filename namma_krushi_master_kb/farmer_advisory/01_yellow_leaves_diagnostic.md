# Yellow leaves: diagnostic guide

“Leaves are yellow” is not a diagnosis. Ask four questions first:

1. Are older/lower leaves affected first, or new leaves?
2. Is yellowing uniform, between veins, around spots, or mixed with mosaic?
3. Are roots healthy and the soil well drained?
4. Are there pests, webbing, leaf curl, wilting or visible lesions?

## Common possibilities

Older-leaf yellowing can occur with normal senescence, nitrogen/magnesium problems, root stress, Fusarium wilt or foliar disease. New-leaf distortion and yellowing with severe curling suggests a virus/vector problem. Yellow halos around spots point toward a leaf-spot disease. Interveinal yellowing may suggest a nutrient-availability issue. Whole-plant pale growth after waterlogging may indicate root-zone dysfunction.
