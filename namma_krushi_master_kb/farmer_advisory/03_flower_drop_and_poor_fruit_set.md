# Flower drop and poor fruit set

Possible causes include heat stress, temperature extremes, high humidity, poor pollen release, water stress, excessive vegetative growth, nutrient imbalance, thrips injury and disease.

## Practical checks

Look at temperature during flowering, ventilation, recent irrigation consistency, pollen release, canopy density and pest presence in flowers. In cloudy or foggy protected conditions, gentle flower-cluster vibration may support pollination. If the problem is concentrated during a hot period, prioritize heat and ventilation management before changing fertilizer.
