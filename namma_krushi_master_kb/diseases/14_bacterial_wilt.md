# Bacterial wilt

Bacterial wilt can cause rapid wilt of an otherwise green plant, sometimes with lower-leaf drooping first. Cut stems may show vascular discoloration and bacterial ooze. High soil moisture can favour disease development.

## Distinguishing clues

Sudden permanent wilt despite adequate water, plus vascular symptoms, is more concerning than temporary midday wilt that recovers in evening. Bacterial wilt can persist in infested soil and spread through contaminated water, soil, tools and planting material.

## Management principles

Use disease-free seedlings, remove infected plants and contaminated root material, sanitize tools, improve drainage, avoid moving infested soil, and rotate where possible. Local resistant rootstocks or cultivars may be part of a long-term strategy.
