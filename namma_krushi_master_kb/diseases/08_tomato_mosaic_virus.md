# Tomato mosaic virus / mosaic disease

Mosaic disease causes irregular light- and dark-green mottling, distortion, puckering and sometimes narrow or fern-like leaflets. Plants may be stunted. Some mosaic viruses can spread mechanically through hands, tools, contaminated debris or planting material.

## Distinguishing clues

Mosaic is a pattern across leaf tissue rather than discrete fungal spots. Nutrient deficiency may cause chlorosis but usually follows vein or leaf-age patterns rather than a patchy mosaic.

## Management principles

Use disease-free seed and seedlings, remove clearly infected plants when recommended, sanitize tools and hands, avoid unnecessary handling, manage weeds and crop residues, and follow hygiene between greenhouse sections.
