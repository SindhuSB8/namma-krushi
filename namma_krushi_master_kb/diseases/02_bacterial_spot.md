# Bacterial spot

Bacterial spot can produce small water-soaked lesions that darken and may develop a yellow halo. Fruit can develop small raised or scabby lesions. Moist weather, persistent dew and splash help spread bacterial inoculum.

## Distinguishing clues

Look for many small lesions rather than large concentric-ring lesions typical of early blight. Ask whether fruit also has rough spots and whether the greenhouse recently had prolonged leaf wetness or splash.

## Management principles

Start with disease-free seed/seedlings, sanitation, removal of badly affected tissue where practical, reduction of splash, and disinfection of tools. Avoid working through wet plants. Because bactericides and pesticide registrations vary, chemical treatment should follow current local guidance rather than a hard-coded chatbot dose.
