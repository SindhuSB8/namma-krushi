# Damping off

Damping off is mainly a nursery problem. Seeds or seedlings may rot before emergence, while emerged seedlings develop soft water-soaked tissue near the base and collapse. Wet, crowded, poorly drained nursery conditions strongly favour the problem.

## Management principles

Use clean trays and media, avoid overwatering, provide drainage and airflow, remove collapsed seedlings, reduce overcrowding and use healthy treated seed according to local nursery recommendations. Once seedlings have collapsed, restoring them is usually not possible; prevention is the main strategy.
