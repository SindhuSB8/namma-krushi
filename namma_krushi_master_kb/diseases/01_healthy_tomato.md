# Healthy tomato leaf

A healthy tomato leaf is typically green, turgid and free from expanding lesions, unusual mosaic patterns, heavy curling, bronzing, webbing or widespread necrosis. Minor old-leaf yellowing, dust, mechanical tears, nutrient variation or insect feeding can occur without representing a major disease.

A CNN prediction of `Healthy` should still be treated as an image classification result, not proof that the entire plant is healthy. The image may not show roots, stems, fruit, lower canopy, mites on the underside of leaves or early symptoms outside the photographed area.

If the farmer reports serious symptoms despite a healthy image classification, ask for another image and symptom description rather than dismissing the concern.
