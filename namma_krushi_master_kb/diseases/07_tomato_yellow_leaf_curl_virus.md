# Tomato yellow leaf curl / leaf curl virus

Leaf curl disease is associated with severe curling or crinkling of leaves, shortened internodes, stunting and a bushy appearance. Whiteflies are important vectors for tomato leaf-curl viruses.

## Distinguishing clues

A plant can curl leaves from heat or water stress, so look for persistent distorted new growth, stunting and whitefly presence. Virus-infected plants do not recover simply by correcting irrigation.

## Management principles

Begin with healthy seedlings, use insect-exclusion practices, monitor whiteflies with sticky traps, remove strongly infected plants early when appropriate, control weed hosts and manage whitefly populations with an IPM program. Virus management focuses heavily on prevention because there is no curative spray that removes virus from an infected plant.
