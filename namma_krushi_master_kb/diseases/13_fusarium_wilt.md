# Fusarium wilt

Fusarium wilt commonly begins with yellowing of lower leaves followed by drooping and progressive wilt. Browning of the vascular tissue inside the stem is an important diagnostic clue. The pathogen survives in soil and can spread through contaminated debris, water and equipment.

## Distinguishing clues

Cut a symptomatic lower stem lengthwise. Brown vascular tissue supports a vascular-wilt diagnosis, whereas simple heat stress usually does not produce vascular browning. Wilt may initially affect one side of a plant.

## Management principles

Use resistant cultivars where available, start with healthy seedlings, rotate with non-hosts, remove infected plants, sanitize tools and manage contaminated soil/media. Avoid moving soil between clean and affected greenhouse zones.
