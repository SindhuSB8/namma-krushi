# Powdery mildew

Powdery mildew can cause yellowish leaf areas and visible white powdery fungal growth. Unlike diseases that require free water on leaves, powdery mildew can develop under relatively dry leaf surfaces when the greenhouse climate otherwise favours the pathogen.

## Distinguishing clues

Look for superficial white growth that can spread across affected areas. Do not confuse mineral deposits from irrigation water with fungal growth; inspect multiple leaves and both surfaces.

## Management principles

Improve sanitation and airflow, remove heavily affected tissue, avoid excessive canopy density and use resistant material when available. If chemical control is required, rotate modes of action according to current local guidance to reduce resistance risk.
