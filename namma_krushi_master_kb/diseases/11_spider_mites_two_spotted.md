# Spider mites / two-spotted spider mite

Spider mites are pests, not fungal diseases. Feeding causes stippling, bronzing or reddish-brown foliage. With heavier infestation, fine webbing may be visible and leaves can dry. Flower and fruit formation can be affected when populations become high.

## Distinguishing clues

Inspect the underside of leaves with a magnifier. Tiny moving mites, eggs and fine webbing strongly support a mite diagnosis. Heat and dry conditions often favour rapid mite multiplication.

## Management principles

Scout early, remove severely infested leaves where practical, reduce plant stress, avoid unnecessary broad-spectrum insecticide use that destroys natural enemies, and use locally recommended biological or acaricide options when thresholds justify treatment.
