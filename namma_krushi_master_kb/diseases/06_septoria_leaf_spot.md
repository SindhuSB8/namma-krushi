# Septoria leaf spot

Septoria produces many small round-to-irregular leaf spots, often with a greyish centre and darker margin. Spots can merge, causing blighting and defoliation. High humidity and infected crop debris contribute to disease development and spread.

## Distinguishing clues

Compared with early blight, Septoria spots are generally smaller and do not show the same prominent concentric-ring pattern. Heavy defoliation can expose fruit to sunscald.

## Management principles

Remove infected debris, reduce splash and prolonged leaf wetness, improve ventilation, and maintain sanitation. Use current local recommendations if a fungicide is required.
