# Late blight

Late blight can progress rapidly under cool-to-mild, wet and humid conditions. Leaves develop expanding dark or water-soaked lesions, and stems and fruit can also be affected. Under very humid conditions, pale fungal growth may be visible near lesion margins, especially on the lower leaf surface.

## Why it matters

Rapid progression distinguishes late blight from many slower leaf spots. A farmer reporting a sudden whole-house increase in dark lesions after prolonged humidity or wet foliage should treat the situation as urgent.

## Management principles

Remove infected tissue when feasible, reduce prolonged leaf wetness, improve ventilation, avoid overhead wetting and follow an integrated disease-management program. Confirm locally before chemical treatment because product approvals and resistance-management recommendations change.
