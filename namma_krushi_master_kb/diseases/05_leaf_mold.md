# Leaf mold

Leaf mold is especially associated with protected cultivation and humid, poorly ventilated canopies. Typical symptoms include pale or yellow patches on the upper leaf surface with olive-green to brown fungal growth on the corresponding lower surface. Older leaves are often affected first.

## Distinguishing clues

The visible fungal growth on the underside and a high-humidity greenhouse history are important clues. It can be confused with nutrient yellowing if the underside is not checked.

## Management principles

Improve ventilation, reduce persistent humidity, avoid dense foliage, remove badly infected leaves and keep plant spacing/training adequate for air movement. Resistant cultivars can be valuable where the disease is recurrent.
