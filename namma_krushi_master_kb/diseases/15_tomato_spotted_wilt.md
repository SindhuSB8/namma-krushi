# Tomato spotted wilt

Tomato spotted wilt virus can cause bronzing, spotting, leaf distortion, stunting and ring-like symptoms on fruit. Thrips are important vectors.

## Management principles

Use clean planting material, exclude and monitor thrips, remove infected plants when appropriate, manage weeds that can host both virus and thrips, and maintain greenhouse sanitation. Because the virus cannot be cured inside an infected plant, vector management and prevention are central.
