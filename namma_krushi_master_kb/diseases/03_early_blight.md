# Early blight

Early blight is associated with `Alternaria` and commonly produces brown to dark lesions that enlarge with concentric rings, giving a target or bull's-eye appearance. Symptoms often begin on older leaves and can be accompanied by yellowing around lesions. Stem and fruit lesions can also occur.

## Favouring conditions

Warm conditions, leaf wetness, high humidity and infected plant debris can increase disease pressure. Dense canopies that stay damp are a management concern.

## Management principles

Remove heavily infected debris, improve airflow, avoid unnecessary leaf wetness, maintain balanced crop nutrition and rotate where possible. Use disease-free planting material. If fungicide intervention is required, use a currently registered product and local extension recommendation.
