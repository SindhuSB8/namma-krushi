# Target spot

“Target spot” is used for circular leaf lesions with concentric zoning caused by certain fungal pathogens. Image datasets may use this as a separate class from early blight even though both can show target-like rings.

## Diagnostic caution

Because visual overlap is strong, a CNN prediction of `Target Spot` should be described as a likely class rather than a laboratory-confirmed diagnosis. Ask whether lesions occur on older foliage, whether fruit/stems are affected, and how quickly the problem is spreading.

## Management principles

Sanitation, removal of heavily infected debris, airflow, avoidance of prolonged leaf wetness and appropriate crop-protection guidance are sensible general measures. For exact pathogen confirmation or chemical choice, consult local plant pathology or horticulture services.
